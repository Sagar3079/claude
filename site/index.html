How a TOLERANCE course is made

Every outline is reviewed by two senior engineers from outside the company before a single lesson is recorded. If they cannot follow the argument, the outline goes back.

Every claim about performance, ergonomics, or cost is benchmarked on hardware described in the lesson. Numbers on screen are numbers you can reproduce.

When the ecosystem moves under a course, the affected modules are re-recorded and republished. You keep access to the new versions at no cost.
